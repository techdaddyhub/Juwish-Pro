Official Data Sovereignty Policy

Privacy Policy & Security Standard

Effective Date: March 2026 | Last Revised: October 2026 | Applicable to: Android App, iOS, Web & Desktop Clients

01. Core Principles & Zero Monetization

At JuwishPro (operated by D. Millers Global Ecosystem), user trust and digital sovereignty are paramount. We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize your personal or financial data to third-party data brokers or advertising networks. Our business model is strictly fee-based from institutional trading execution and staking validation services.

All personal identifying data (PII) is encrypted at rest using military-grade AES-256 encryption and in transit using TLS 1.3 cryptographic tunnels.

02. Information We Collect

To provide secure multi-asset trading, portfolio tracking, and decentralized staking via our mobile application and platform services, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, verified email address, phone number, and authentication tokens.
  • Financial & Transaction Data: Public cryptographic wallet addresses, JuwishCoin (JWC) deposit transactions, deposit receipts, withdrawal records, and order histories. We never store private keys.
  • Device & Security Telemetry: Device identifiers, operating system version, and IP addresses exclusively used for brute-force protection, account hijacking defense, and regulatory AML compliance.

03. Purpose of Data Processing

We collect and process your information solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Executing market orders, crypto swap settlements, and high-yield staking dividends.
  • Confirming instant JuwishCoin (JWC) purchases at the PancakeSwap live valuation ($3.00 USD).
  • Providing real-time order notifications, trade fills, and multi-factor authentication (MFA) codes.
  • Preventing fraud, mitigating money laundering risks, and complying with international financial directives (GDPR, CCPA).

04. Permissions Required by the Android Mobile App

The JuwishPro Android Client application (org.dmillers.juwishpro) requests minimal permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to connect to real-time ticker feeds, PancakeSwap liquidity pools, and secure order execution.
  • CAMERA (Optional): Used exclusively within the QR code scanner to scan recipient wallet addresses and deposit payment addresses. No video recordings are stored.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional): Used to deliver instant transaction alerts, fill notices, and security advisories.

05. Data Retention & User Rights (Account Deletion)

In compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies and GDPR, every user possesses full authority over their personal data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: You may request an export of all transaction histories and account records.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can request full deletion of your user account and personal data at any time directly through the app settings or by emailing support@juwishcoin.online or info@juwishcoin.online. Deletion will be completed within 14 business days.
