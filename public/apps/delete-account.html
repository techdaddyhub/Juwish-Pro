User Data Rights & Account Removal

Account & Personal Data Deletion

Applies to Juwishcoin / JuwishPro (org.dmillers.juwishpro) operated by D. Millers Global Ecosystem.

Option 1: Delete Your Account Inside the App

  1. Open the JuwishPro / Juwishcoin Android, Desktop, or Web app and sign in.
  2. Open the navigation menu and select Profile Settings (/user/account/profile).
  3. Scroll to the Delete Account section at the bottom of the page.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm with your account password to immediately deactivate your account, revoke all active sessions, and purge your personal profile details.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Web (No Login Required)

If you cannot access your account or have uninstalled the app, submit the request below. Our Data Protection Officer will process deletion within 14 business days and send confirmation to your registered email.

Or email info@juwishcoin.online directly

Data Types Deleted vs. Statutory Retention

  • Immediately Anonymized / Deleted: Account credentials, name, email address, username, profile photo, uploaded KYC images, session tokens, and device identifiers.
  • Statutory Financial Compliance Retention: Where required by anti-money-laundering (AML) or tax regulations, anonymized financial transaction and blockchain ledger timestamps may be retained securely in cold audit logs for up to 5 years before automatic purge.
